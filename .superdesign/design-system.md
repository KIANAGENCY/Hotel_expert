# Hotel Expert — Sistema ELAH design system

## Product and job
Hotel Expert is a B2B hotel operations brand. Sistema ELAH means “Estandarización de Limpieza y Aroma en Hoteles.” The primary comprehension device is “LIMPIEZA + AROMA = ELAH”. The journey is Home → ELAH → Products → Sample.

## Visual direction
Evolve, do not replace, the existing identity. Professional hotelier, clean, elegant, technological and subtly sensory. Editorial compositions, strong hierarchy and ample negative space. Avoid domestic cleaning, perfume boutique, spa and ecommerce aesthetics. One central idea per section, using diagrams and statements before cards.

## Brand constraints
Use the real Hotel Expert logo. Never redraw or substitute it. Use only the existing palette: #0B2345 anchor, #008C95 and #52C8C8 accents, #EAF5F5 and #F3F0EA soft surfaces, #222326 text, white. Use Montserrat for display/CTA and Source Sans 3 for body. Preserve recognizable pill-like CTA style while improving spacing and focus states.

## Typography
Large ELAH display can use clamp(4.75rem, 15vw, 10rem), compact line-height, strong weight. H2 2.25–4.5rem. Body 1–1.2rem, line-height 1.65, measure 60–68ch. Avoid excessive uppercase outside eyebrows/CTA.

## Layout and responsive
Mobile first. Header on mobile: logo + visible sample CTA + hamburger. Desktop navigation: ELAH, Products, Aroma insignia, Resources, About, Contact. Home is a flowing story with varied layouts, not repeated card grids. Comparisons stack into product columns on narrow screens. Touch targets minimum 44px.

## Components
Use semantic sections, accessible accordions, real labels, clear focus-visible outlines and descriptive links. Reuse shared header/footer/form/CTA patterns. WhatsApp remains visible but non-invasive.

## Motion
Subtle one-time reveal, 180–500ms hover/accordion feedback. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No parallax or scroll hijacking.

## Content constraints
Do not invent slogans, metrics, certifications, clients, testimonials, prices or social URLs. Keep exact ELAH definition. Dual adds neutralization of bad odors. Hotel Expert dilution is 1:9 and 2 L makes 20 L. Do not use on glass or mirrors.

## Target Home structure
Hero with SISTEMA / ELAH / definition / equation and two CTAs; two-world equation; before-vs-ELAH identity diagram; three-step operation; housekeeping differentiation statement; two core products; responsive comparison; hotel-space visualization; aroma-insignia editorial section; four operational data points; operation-to-guest sequence; conversion; accessible FAQ; sparse ELAH close.
